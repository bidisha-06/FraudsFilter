      DIGITAL SAFETY

      Online Safety Guide

      Follow these basic practices before trusting an online link.

      🔗

      Check the URL

      Look for misspellings, unusual domains and misleading website names.

      🔐

      Protect Credentials

      Never share OTPs, passwords or banking information through suspicious links.

      ⚠️

      Avoid Urgency

      Be careful with messages demanding immediate payment or account verification.

      ✓

      Verify Independently

      Visit official websites directly instead of trusting unexpected messages.
