🚨 Phone Scam Red Flags

  • Demanding OTP / PIN: Banks never ask for OTPs over a call.
  • Digital Arrest: No law allows arrest via phone or video call.
  • Screen Sharing: Never install AnyDesk/TeamViewer for callers.
  • Call Pressure: Scammers demand that you do not disconnect.
